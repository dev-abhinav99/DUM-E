DUM-E: AI-Powered Dual-Arm Mobile Household Assistant

Elderly people and people with limited mobility spend real effort on repetitive chores. DUM-E listens to a spoken request, then collects scattered objects, brings a water bottle, or organizes belongings into a container, so daily life needs less lifting and bending.
